## What's a Proof?

A proof is every signed answer we mint: a verification, an approval or a denial, a Proof of Me answer, a delegation. Proofs for your own account count like any other: each phone, email or domain you add, and each domain renewal. Signing in reuses your existing proof until it is six months old, so it is re-issued, and counted again, at most about twice a year.

- ✅ Phone number verified via Telegram → 1 proof
- ✅ Phone number verified via WhatsApp → 1 proof
- ✅ Phone number verified via SMS → 1 proof
- ✅ Email verified via magic link → 1 proof
- ✅ Domain verified via DNS → 1 proof
- ✅ Approval request approved or denied → 1 proof
- ✅ Proof of Me answer → 1 proof
- ✅ Delegation issued → 1 proof
- ✅ Phone verified and then refused by your app (the relying party's outcome) → 1 proof: the proof was delivered, and the refusal is your decision
- ✅ Social account verified via OAuth → 1 proof (coming soon)
- ✅ Wallet verified via signature → 1 proof (coming soon)

**What's NOT counted:**
- ❌ Failed verification attempts
- ❌ Expired challenges (not completed)
- ❌ Test mode verifications (`pk_test_` keys — unlimited, free)
- ❌ API calls (free; your plan's per-second rate limit applies)
- ❌ Webhook deliveries (unlimited)
- ❌ Proof validations (unlimited, offline)

## FAQ

### How does billing work?
- Plans are charged in advance for each billing period
- Proofs beyond your plan come from a prepaid balance you top up in the dashboard
- No long-term contracts

### Can I change plans?
- Upgrade anytime, prorated
- Downgrade at end of billing cycle
- No penalties

### Do unused proofs roll over?
- No, included proofs reset monthly
- PAYG overage has no expiration

### Is there a free trial?
- PAYG includes 300 free proofs/month
- No credit card required to start
- Test mode (`pk_test_` keys) is always free with unlimited verifications

### What exactly is test mode?
- Use `pk_test_` API keys instead of `pk_live_`
- Direct verifications send no email and skip the DNS and HTTP checks
- Use the `test-verify` endpoint to complete verifications instantly
- Approval messages and verification-request emails are still delivered for real
- Test verifications don't count toward your monthly quota or billing
- Request and approval webhooks still fire, so you can test your integration end to end

### What's the difference between the €79/month and Custom plans?
- **€79/month**: Custom branding in messages, shared infrastructure
- **Custom**: on request, dedicated infrastructure and your own Telegram bot or WhatsApp number (BYOC)
